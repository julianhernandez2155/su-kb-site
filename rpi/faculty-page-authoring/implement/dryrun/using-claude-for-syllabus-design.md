Claude can help you turn a rough course outline into a polished syllabus draft in minutes —
while you stay in control of every academic decision. This page walks through one practical
workflow for Syracuse instructors.

> [!note] Claude drafts, you decide
> Always review generated policies, dates, and learning outcomes against your department's
> requirements before you publish a syllabus to students.

## Before you start

You'll need a Syracuse Claude account (sign in with your NetID) and a rough idea of the course:

- **Course basics**: title, level, credit hours, and meeting pattern.
- **Topics**: the main units or themes you want to cover.
- **Last term's syllabus** (optional): paste it in to give Claude a concrete starting point.

For what course material is appropriate to share with an AI tool, see
[Approved Tools for Use with University Data](../../../data-ai/ai-general-information/approved-tools-for-use-with-university-data.md).

---

## Steps

1. **Describe the course** in a new chat — level, credit hours, weekly meeting pattern — and ask
   Claude to propose a topic sequence across the term.
2. **Draft the standard sections.** Ask for the course description, learning outcomes, grading
   breakdown, and a week-by-week schedule. Paste in any required boilerplate (academic integrity,
   accessibility, religious-observance language) so the draft uses Syracuse's wording.
3. **Refine section by section** with follow-up prompts, for example:
   - "Rewrite the learning outcomes as measurable statements starting with an action verb."
   - "Check this schedule for an even workload and flag any week that looks overloaded."
4. **Accessibility-check the language.** Ask Claude to review the document for plain-language
   clarity so it's readable for every student in the course.

---

## Keep in mind

- **📝 You own the academic decisions.** Claude structures and drafts; you verify accuracy, tone,
  and policy compliance.
- **🚫 No confidential data.** Never paste student records, grades, or FERPA-protected details
  into the draft.
- **📅 Verify dates.** Check every deadline against the official academic calendar before you
  share the syllabus.
